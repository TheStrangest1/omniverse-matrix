# OMNIVERSE-MATRIX — Stage 3 Research Progress

**Research checkpoint** | 2 November 2026  
**Status:** Stage 3, Questions 1–4 completed  
**Manuscript:** unchanged (historical working draft remains v0.4.7)

## Current framework architecture

The current working architecture retains three fundamental physical variables:

- **Matter**
- **Energy**
- **Mass**

Time is not treated as a fourth fundamental variable. Temporal succession is being investigated as an emergent or relational feature rather than a fundamental coordinate.

The broader OMNIVERSE-MATRIX proposal remains a speculative thought experiment. Its metaphysical and ontological postulates have not been established by the mathematical investigations.

## Stage 3 — Four research foundations

### 1. Timeless containment
A fixed mathematical structure can contain multiple configurations or represent complete histories without itself evolving.

**Limitation:** Mathematical representation does not establish that every represented configuration is physically realized.

### 2. Non-permanent splits
Existing mathematical structures can represent alternative configurations, divergent paths, shared origins, and possible convergence. Separate branches are not necessarily permanent or isolated.

**Limitation:** A mathematical split does not establish a physical branching mechanism.

### 3. Null-configuration possibility
The absence of localized Matter, Energy, and Mass does not logically entail the absence of underlying structure.

**Limitation:** An explicit mathematical construction of a null configuration within the proposed architecture remains open.

### 4. Multi-node overlap
Multiple nodes can relate to overlapping or distinct configurations within one shared structure. Their accessible subsets need not be disjoint.

**Limitation:** Describing possible patterns of access does not explain what governs access.

## Central unresolved problem: Localization

We can describe how multiple nodes may access a shared structure, but we have not yet explained what governs that access.

The next investigation will examine existing mathematical selection and restriction mechanisms before proposing any framework-specific mechanism.

## Research constraints

- Preserve the three-variable architecture: Matter, Energy, Mass.
- Do not introduce Time or consciousness as a fourth fundamental physical variable.
- Do not assume that nodes choose their configurations.
- Do not assume that consciousness determines physical reality.
- Do not assume that the Source requires a local observer.
- Distinguish established mathematics and physics from proposed extensions and philosophical ontology.
- Do not treat mathematical accessibility as proof of physical realization.
